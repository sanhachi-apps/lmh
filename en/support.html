Reporting a Problem

Please include the following information where possible. You can omit anything you do not know.

  • Your device model.
  • Your operating system and version, such as iOS or Android. The operating system is the basic software that runs your device.
  • Your app version, which identifies the app release.
  • The steps you took before the problem occurred.
  • What you expected to happen and what actually happened.
  • The text of any error message displayed.

The App does not automatically send photos, measurement records, or crash reports to the Developer. Crash reports describe unexpected app shutdowns. The information you provide helps the Developer investigate the problem.

Information You Should Not Send

Do not send passwords, verification codes, card numbers, addresses, phone numbers, or other information unnecessary for your inquiry. The Developer will not ask for your Apple or Google account password.

Before attaching a photo or screenshot, check for real names, email addresses, other people's information, or business secrets, and hide them where necessary. Attaching the original measurement photo is not required.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are the area measurements accurate?

Measurements are estimates calculated from photos and are provided for reference only. Accuracy is not guaranteed. Do not use them for sales, transactions, certification, inspections, or other purposes requiring accurate measurements. See the Disclaimer for details.

Why do the results change between measurements?

Camera tilt, the specified reference-sheet corners, the paper-placement setting, lens distortion, and positioning the reference sheet away from the center of the frame can cause large differences in results. Check the shooting conditions and selected positions and regions. Automatically detected or selected results should also be checked. In Rapid mode and S-Rapid mode, the displayed area also tends to vary more from shot to shot than in Standard mode.

Are my photos or measurement results sent to the Developer?

The App does not send photos, measurement results, tilt information, or similar measurement data to the Developer or third parties. If you choose to use CSV export, exported data is passed to the sharing destination you select. Saved records may also be included in your device backup, depending on your settings. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Are measurement records saved automatically?

No. Records are saved in the App's dedicated storage area on your device only when you press the Save button. The free version can store up to 3 records, and Pro can store up to 50 records.

Do I need Pro for automatic reference-sheet detection?

No. Automatic reference-sheet detection is available in the free version. Pro is a one-time paid upgrade that includes Rapid mode, S-Rapid mode, automatic leather-region selection, CSV export, and an increased record storage limit.

Can the Developer restore deleted records?

No. The Developer does not keep your measurement records and cannot restore them from the Developer's systems. Whether records can be restored from a device backup depends on the backup's contents and device settings. Restoration cannot be guaranteed.

Who should I contact about purchases or billing?

For questions about Pro features, please use the email address above. Payments are processed by the app store, and the Developer does not receive payment details such as your card number. For billing or refund procedures, please consult the app store where you made the purchase.
