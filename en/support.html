Android Requirements

The Android version is intended for devices running Android 7.0 (API level 24) or later that can use Google Play. An API level is a number identifying an Android platform version.

Auto-select for leather is included in Pro, a one-time paid upgrade. It normally works on devices that installed the app from Google Play. An internet connection is needed the first time you use it. Painting by hand with “Paint” is free to use.

Depending on your device environment and network conditions, automatic leather selection may be unavailable. Purchasing Pro does not remove these environmental limitations.

Reporting a Problem

Please include the following information where possible. You can omit anything you do not know.

  • Whether you are using the iPhone or Android version.
  • Your device model.
  • Your operating system and version, such as iOS or Android. The operating system is the basic software that runs your device.
  • Your app version, which identifies the app release. On Android, you can find it in Settings under “About this app.”
  • The steps you took before the problem occurred.
  • What you expected to happen and what actually happened.
  • The text of any error message displayed.

The App does not automatically send photos or measurement records to the Developer. The information you provide helps the Developer investigate the problem. Please see the Privacy Policy for information about diagnostic data that ML Kit (Google's component for image recognition and similar features) in the Android version may send to Google.

Do not send passwords, verification codes, card numbers, addresses, phone numbers, or other information unnecessary for your inquiry. The Developer will not ask for your Apple or Google account password.

Before attaching a photo or screenshot, check for real names, email addresses, other people's information, or business secrets, and hide them where necessary. Attaching the original measurement photo is not required.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are the area measurements accurate?

Measurements are estimates calculated from photos and are provided for reference only. Accuracy is not guaranteed. Do not use them for sales, transactions, certification, inspections, or other purposes requiring accurate measurements. The area shown on the camera view before shooting is also an approximate reference value. See the Disclaimer for details.

Why do the results change between measurements?

Camera tilt, the specified reference-sheet corners, the paper-placement setting, lens distortion, and positioning the reference sheet away from the center of the frame can cause large differences in results. Check the shooting conditions and selected positions and regions. Automatically detected or selected results should also be checked.

In Rapid and S-Rapid, the displayed area varies more from shot to shot than in Standard mode. The preview shown before shooting in S-Rapid varies the most.

On Android, differences in device models, cameras, and image processing used when shooting may also affect measurements and the results of automatic detection or selection of the reference sheet and the leather.

Are my photos or measurement results sent to the Developer?

The App does not send photos, camera footage, measurement results, tilt information, or similar measurement data to the Developer's servers. The analysis of the camera view before shooting in S-Rapid is also performed on your device, and the video is neither saved nor sent.

ML Kit in the Android version also analyzes photos on your device and does not send your photos or camera footage to Google's servers for that analysis. However, ML Kit may send diagnostic information to Google separately from your photos and measurement results. For the types of information sent, the conditions for sending it, and encryption of the communication, please consult Google's official documentation linked in the Privacy Policy.

If you choose to use CSV export, exported data is passed to the sharing destination you select. See the Privacy Policy for details, including information about backups and device transfers.

Are measurement records saved automatically?

No. Records are saved in the App's dedicated storage area on your device only when you press the Save button. The free version can store up to 3 records, and Pro can store up to 50 records.

Do I need Pro for automatic reference-sheet detection?

No. Automatic reference-sheet detection is available in the free version. Pro is a one-time paid upgrade that includes Rapid and S-Rapid, automatic leather-region selection, CSV export, and an increased record storage limit. Purchasing Pro does not guarantee measurement accuracy.

What do I need when using automatic leather selection for the first time on Android?

Auto-select for leather is included in Pro, a one-time paid upgrade. It normally works on devices that installed the app from Google Play. An internet connection is needed the first time you use it. Painting by hand with “Paint” is free to use.

Does S-Rapid make my device warm or drain the battery?

S-Rapid keeps analyzing the camera view before you shoot, so battery use and heat may increase. If this is a concern, lengthen the update interval in the menu on the camera screen, or switch to Standard mode or Rapid.

Why is Pro unavailable after I purchased it on Android?

Pro is a one-time purchase. On Android, it is available with the Google Account used for the purchase, based on purchase information from Google Play. If Pro is not available, please try “Restore Purchases” in the App.

If the problem continues, please contact support with your device model, Android version, app version, and any displayed message. Do not send your Google Account password or card number.

Can the Developer restore deleted records?

No. The Developer does not keep your measurement records and cannot restore them from the Developer's systems.

On iPhone, saved records may be included in device backups depending on device settings and other conditions. Whether records can be restored depends on the backup's contents and device settings, and restoration cannot be guaranteed. The Android version is configured to disable Android Auto Backup.

Whether app settings and saved records are transferred through computer-based backups, data migration tools provided by device manufacturers, or similar methods depends on the specifications of the device and software. They may not be transferred.

If you want to retain information from your saved records, please use CSV export, which is included in Pro. However, CSV export does not guarantee a complete backup of your records, including photos, or restoration into the App. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Who should I contact about purchases or billing?

For questions about Pro features, please use the email address above. Purchases and payments are processed by Apple for the iPhone version and Google Play for the Android version. The Developer does not receive payment details such as your card number. For billing inquiries and procedures, please consult the app store where you made the purchase.

Where can I ask about refunds?

Refunds for the Android version are handled under Google Play's refund rules and procedures. Conditions may vary depending on the timing of purchase, your region, and other factors. If you have any questions, please contact support.

For the iPhone version, please consult Apple's refund information.
