Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled in Leather Measurement Helper (LMH; Japanese name: レザー面積測る君, referred to as “the App”), independently developed by sanhachi leather (サンハチレザー, referred to as “the Developer”). This policy applies to both the iPhone and Android versions. Where their practices differ, the relevant version is identified.

1. General Policy

The App does not send photos, camera footage, measurement results, tilt information, or similar data it handles for measurement to the Developer's servers. The Developer does not operate a server that receives this data.

No account registration is required. The App does not use SDKs (external software components added to an app) for advertising. The iPhone version also does not use SDKs for usage analytics or crash reporting (reports about unexpected app shutdowns).

For information about diagnostic data handled by Google ML Kit in the Android version, please see “7. Google ML Kit in the Android Version.” Information about user-selected CSV sharing destinations, device backups, app-store purchases, support inquiries, and visits to this website is provided below.

2. Device Features and Their Purposes

  • Camera: Used to photograph leather and a reference sheet so the App can estimate an area from the photo. Automatic detection of the reference sheet and automatic selection of the leather are performed entirely on your device, and photos are not sent anywhere. In S-Rapid, the camera view is analyzed on your device before you press the shutter to show candidates for the reference sheet and the leather. This video is not saved and is not sent to the Developer or any third party.
  • Photo library: The App reads only the photos you select for measurement. It does not read your entire photo library.
  • Tilt sensor: Used to measure the tilt of your phone when you take a photo, and to save that tilt in the measurement records you choose to save. Whether the tilt is shown on the camera screen can be switched in Settings (it is not shown by default).

Permission requests for features such as the camera are handled through your device's permission system. You can change permissions in your device settings. If you do not grant a required permission, the corresponding feature may be unavailable.

3. Saving Measurement Records

The App saves measurement records in its dedicated storage area on your device only when you press the Save button. It does not save measurement records automatically.

Saved information includes:

  • Record name, notes, date and time, and area.
  • Reference-sheet type and orientation, the positions of its four corners, and the paper-placement setting.
  • Tilt information and the lens used.
  • A reduced-size copy of the original photo, an image of the marked region, and a thumbnail, which is a small image used in record lists.

Record names and notes contain information you enter. Please avoid entering unnecessary personal information about other people or other information you do not need to save.

4. Deletion and Backups

You can delete saved records within the App. Deleting the App from your device also deletes records stored in the App's dedicated storage area on that device. However, this does not delete copies in the backups described below or data you have exported elsewhere.

iPhone version:
If you use a device backup feature, such as iCloud Backup, saved records may be included in the backup. A backup is a copy of device data stored in another location. Backup creation, storage, restoration, and deletion depend on your device and the settings of the service you use. The Developer does not obtain or view the contents of your backups. Copies may remain in backups created before you delete records or the App.

Android version:
The Android version is configured to disable Android Auto Backup.

Computer-based backups, migration tools, and similar methods:
Whether app settings and saved records are transferred by backups made using a computer, data migration tools provided by device manufacturers, or similar methods depends on the specifications of the device and software. They may not be transferred. The Developer does not obtain or view the contents of these backups or migration tools.

If you want to retain information from your saved records, please use CSV export, which is included in Pro, a one-time paid upgrade. However, CSV export does not guarantee a complete backup of your records, including photos, or restoration into the App. Please manage and delete exported data and backup copies held by the relevant services at their respective storage destinations.

5. CSV Export and Sharing

CSV export, included in Pro, outputs measurement records in a file format usable with spreadsheet software and similar tools. When you choose to export and share data, it is passed only to the sharing destination you select. This action does not send the data to the Developer.

Data passed to a sharing destination is subject to that destination's terms and privacy policy. Please check both the data and its destination before sharing.

6. In-App Purchase

The App offers a one-time in-app purchase called Pro. Purchases and payments are processed by the app store from which you obtained the App: Apple for the iPhone version and Google Play for the Android version. The Developer does not receive payment details such as your card number.

Please consult the relevant app store's terms and privacy policy for information about its handling of purchase data. The App not transmitting measurement data is separate from the app store processing information for purchases or app distribution.

7. Google ML Kit in the Android Version

In the Android version, Pro, a one-time paid upgrade, includes automatic leather-region selection, which uses Google's ML Kit (a component for adding image recognition and similar features to apps). ML Kit analyzes photos on your device, and your photos and camera footage are not sent to Google's servers for that analysis.

ML Kit may send information such as device information, app information, an identifier for each installation, performance statistics, and error codes to Google for diagnostics. This information is separate from your photos and measurement results. For the types of information sent, the conditions for sending it, and encryption of the communication, please refer to Google's published documentation:

https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure

Automatic leather-region selection uses an internet connection, for example the first time you use it, to download data used for analysis (a model).

8. Support Inquiries

If you contact the Developer by email, the Developer receives the information included in your email, such as your email address, sender name, message, and attachments. This information is used to respond to your inquiry and to investigate and address reported problems.

Google Gmail is used to receive support inquiries, and email information is processed and stored through that service. Emails received at the support address are also forwarded to another email inbox controlled by the Developer. Inquiry information is kept for as long as needed to handle the matter and deleted as appropriate when it is no longer needed.

Do not send passwords, verification codes, card numbers, addresses, or other information unnecessary for your inquiry. Before attaching photos or measurement records, please check that they do not contain personal or confidential information.

Except where required by law or otherwise legally permitted, inquiry information is not provided to third parties for purposes other than using services necessary to handle the inquiry. To request deletion of your inquiry information or discuss its handling, please use the contact address below.

9. This Website

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. No advertising or usage analytics tools have been added to this website. However, GitHub, as the hosting provider, may process access information such as IP addresses to deliver the website and operate it securely. Please consult GitHub's published privacy information for details.

10. Children

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children. The Developer does not knowingly collect children's personal information.
