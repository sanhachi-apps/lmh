Leather Measurement Helper

LMH / レザー面積測る君

Estimate leather area from a photo of leather and a reference sheet, such as A4 paper. This app is independently developed by sanhachi leather and supports Japanese and English.

What the App Does

Using the four corners of a reference sheet, the app corrects the photo's perspective to create a view similar to looking straight down at a flat surface. It then calculates the area of the region you mark and displays the result in square decimeters (dm², also called “deshi”) or square feet (sqft).

  • Measure using a photo taken with the camera or a photo you select from your photo library.
  • Automatic reference-sheet detection is available in the free version.
  • View your phone's tilt while taking a photo and include the tilt information in a saved measurement record.
  • Measurement records are saved on your device only when you press the Save button. There is no automatic saving.
  • You can delete saved records.

Device Features Used

  • The camera is used to take measurement photos. In S-Rapid, the camera view is analyzed on your device before you press the shutter to show candidates for the reference sheet and the leather. This video is not saved and is not sent to the developer or any third party.
  • The app reads only the photos you select from your photo library. It does not read your entire photo library.
  • The tilt sensor is used to display and record your phone's tilt when taking a photo.

Free Version and Pro

The free version includes automatic reference-sheet detection, area calculation, and storage for up to 3 measurement records.

Pro is a one-time in-app purchase. It adds Rapid and S-Rapid, automatic selection of the leather region, CSV export, and storage for up to 50 measurement records. CSV is a file format that can be used with spreadsheet software and similar tools.

Rapid finds the reference sheet and the leather automatically right after you shoot and shows the area. S-Rapid overlays candidates for the reference sheet and the leather, plus an approximate area, on the camera view even before you shoot. In both, you can correct the reference sheet and the leather region on the result screen.

The areas shown in Rapid and S-Rapid vary more from shot to shot than in Standard mode. The preview shown before shooting in S-Rapid varies the most. Please use them only as a rough guide.

Photos and Measurement Records

The app does not send photos, measurement results, tilt information, or similar measurement data to the developer or third parties. No account registration is required. The app contains no advertising or usage analytics tools.

If you use CSV export, the exported data is passed only to the sharing destination you choose. Saved records may also be included in your device backup. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Important Limitations

Measurements are estimates calculated from photos and are provided for reference only. Accuracy is not guaranteed. Do not use them for sales, transactions, certification, inspections, or other purposes requiring accurate measurements. Figures in Rapid and S-Rapid vary more than in Standard mode.

Results may differ substantially from the actual area because of camera tilt, incorrect reference-sheet corner positions, an incorrect paper-placement setting, lens distortion, or other factors. Please read the Disclaimer before using the app.
