Disclaimer

This disclaimer applies to the use of Leather Measurement Helper (LMH; Japanese name: レザー面積測る君, referred to as “the App”), independently developed by sanhachi leather (サンハチレザー, referred to as “the Developer”). Please read the following before using the App.

1. Measurements Are Estimates for Reference Only

The App uses a photo of leather and a reference sheet, corrects the photo's perspective based on the four corners of the sheet, and calculates the area of a region specified by the user.

The displayed area is an estimate calculated from a photo and is provided for reference only. It is not guaranteed to match the actual area or meet any particular level of accuracy. Results may vary with shooting conditions and user input, even when measuring the same object. The area shown on the camera view before shooting is also an approximate reference value. Accuracy is not guaranteed. Not for use in sales or transactions. In Rapid mode and S-Rapid mode, the displayed area tends to vary more from shot to shot than in Standard mode.

2. Not for Purposes Requiring Accurate Measurements

Do not use the App's measurements for sales, transactions, certification, inspections, or other purposes requiring accurate measurements. Do not rely on them to determine final sale prices, confirm delivered quantities, or guarantee quality.

If you need an accurate area measurement, use methods or equipment suitable for that purpose and consult an appropriate professional when necessary.

3. Main Factors That Can Cause Large Errors

The following conditions and inputs can cause results to differ substantially from the actual area. These are examples, not an exhaustive list of possible sources of error.

  • Camera tilt: Tilting the phone while taking a photo can affect perspective correction and area calculation. The tilt display is a guide and does not guarantee accuracy.
  • Incorrect reference-sheet corner positions: If automatically detected or manually specified corners do not match the actual corners, perspective correction and area calculation will be inaccurate.
  • Incorrect paper-placement setting: Results can be substantially wrong if the selected setting does not match whether the reference sheet is placed on top of the leather or beside it.
  • Lens distortion: Lens distortion can alter the shapes shown in the photo and affect results. Errors can be especially large with ultra-wide-angle lenses.
  • A reference sheet away from the center: Results may differ substantially when the reference sheet is positioned away from the center of the camera frame.
  • Other shooting conditions and region selection: Curved or wrinkled leather or paper, differences in height, shadows, reflections, blur, an incorrect reference-sheet size setting, and an incorrectly marked region can also affect results.

4. Check Automatically Detected or Selected Regions

Automatic reference-sheet detection and Pro's automatic leather-region selection are not guaranteed to identify the correct positions or regions every time. Check automatic results yourself and correct them where necessary. Purchasing Pro does not guarantee measurement accuracy.

5. Use at Your Own Responsibility

Use the App and make decisions based on its estimates at your own responsibility, with an understanding of this disclaimer and the limitations of its measurement method.

6. Saving Records and Possible Data Loss

The App saves measurement records on your device only when you press the Save button. It does not save automatically. Unsaved measurement information may be lost.

Saved records may also be lost because of deletion by the user, deletion of the App, device failure or loss, or other circumstances. The Developer does not operate a server that receives measurement records and cannot restore lost records from the Developer's systems.

Even if records are included in a device backup, the Developer does not guarantee that a backup can be created or restored. Please also read the Privacy Policy for information about saving, deletion, backups, and exported data.
